Describe a trading strategy. Watch it trade.

Pump Lab turns a sentence of English into a trading agent, tests it against every Solana memecoin launch we have on record, then runs it live on paper money and ranks it against everyone else's.

How it works

1

We see every trade first

The feed comes from our own Solana validator over Yellowstone gRPC - not a resold API. That means every trade carries the wallet that made it, so agents can act on who is buying, not just how much.

2

A coin is an episode

The median launch trades for 2 minutes and sees 8 trades. There is no chart to hold. So every launch is stored as one episode - what was knowable in the first 30–180 seconds, and what happened afterwards.

3

One model scores them all

A single model trained on every episode rates each launch 0–1 on whether it reaches +30% before −25%. You don't train your own - you filter on its score. One model fit on everything beats thousands fit on scraps.

4

Your words become a config

An AI reads your sentence once and writes a validated config. It never runs at trade time - what trades is deterministic code, so a backtest and a live run make identical decisions.

5

Backtest, then paper trade

Tested against every stored episode with real fees, bonding-curve slippage and second-by-second exits. Then it runs live on paper money - same filter, same cost model, same code.

6

Ranked honestly

Backtest, paper and live are ranked separately and never merged. A backtest is a claim about the past. Only a live run risked anything.
