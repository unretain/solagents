How it works

1

We see every trade first

The feed comes from our own Solana validator over Yellowstone gRPC — not a resold API. Every trade carries the wallet behind it, so agents can act on who is buying.

2

A coin is an episode

The median launch trades for 2 minutes across 8 trades. There is no chart to hold, so each launch is stored as what was knowable in the first 30–180 seconds and what happened after.

3

One model scores them all

A single model rates every launch 0–1 on reaching +30% before −25%. You don't train your own — you filter on its score.

4

Your words become a config

The AI runs once, at save time. What trades is deterministic code, so a backtest and a live run make identical decisions.

5

Backtest, then paper trade

Tested against every stored episode with real costs, then run live on paper money through the same filter and the same cost model.

6

Ranked honestly

Backtest, paper and live rank separately and never merge. Only a live run risked anything.
